# Thermal Physics Integration

Added a complete native Thermal Physics learning sequence across the existing EduVerse Physics curriculum.

## Lessons

1. Brownian Motion and Molecular Randomness
2. Ideal Gas Laws and Kinetic-Theory Postulates
3. Kinetic Theory and Pressure Derivation
4. RMS Speed, Temperature and Translational Kinetic Energy
5. Boltzmann Distribution and Statistical Physics
6. Stellar Physics and Evolution
7. Neutron Stars, Pulsars and the Life Cycle of the Sun
8. Thermal Physics Comprehensive Review

## Interactive modes

- Brownian motion
- Ideal-gas particle model and gas-law calculator
- Molecular pressure and wall-collision model
- RMS speed and kinetic-energy explorer
- Boltzmann population explorer
- Stellar hydrostatic-equilibrium model
- Stellar evolution sequence
- Neutron-star and pulsar model
- Integrated thermal-physics review calculator
