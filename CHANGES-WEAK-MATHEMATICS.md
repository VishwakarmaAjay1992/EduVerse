# Weak Mathematics Chapter Completion

This update upgrades every Mathematics chapter previously marked **Weak** to the current EduVerse Strong standard.

## Scope

- 21 former Weak chapters
- 153 existing lessons enriched
- 21 Expert Mastery Review lessons added

## New implementation files

- `src/lib/weak-enrichment.ts`
- `src/content/mathematics/weak-chapter-reviews.ts`
- `tests/unit/weak-mathematics-enrichment.test.ts`
- `documentation/WEAK-CHAPTER-ENRICHMENT-REPORT.md`

## Enrichment standard

Every targeted original lesson now resolves with:

- structured theory and expert reasoning guidance
- at least three worked examples
- visual support through diagrams, tables, cards or interactives
- practice with feedback
- a graded mastery assessment
- summary and completion tracking

Every targeted chapter now also contains a visible Expert Mastery Review lesson.
