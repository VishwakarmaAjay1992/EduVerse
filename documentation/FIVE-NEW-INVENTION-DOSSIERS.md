# Five new invention dossiers

The Human History Inventions collection now contains ten complete routes. The second group adds:

- `/inventions/telephone`
- `/inventions/automobile`
- `/inventions/mechanical-refrigeration`
- `/inventions/transistor`
- `/inventions/programmable-computer`

Each route follows the complete EduVerse invention standard:

1. Necessity and social pressure
2. Earlier methods and global prior art
3. Contributors and disputed credit
4. Experiments, failures and engineering lessons
5. Milestone timeline
6. Step-by-step working principle
7. Component anatomy and design constraints
8. Later improvements
9. Licensed historical-image gallery
10. Benefits, costs and unintended consequences
11. Modern descendants and continuing influence
12. Myths, glossary, research questions and selected sources

The new material prioritizes institutional sources such as the Library of Congress, Smithsonian museums, the German Patent and Trade Mark Office, ASHRAE, Nobel Prize records and the Computer History Museum. Historical images use Wikimedia Commons file records with visible creator, licence and source attribution.

Dedicated visual system diagrams were added for the telephone signal chain, automobile powertrain, vapor-compression refrigeration cycle, transistor control principle and stored-program computer architecture.
