## Coverage decisions

- Repeated definitions, formulae and questions were merged without displaying source-page order.
- Publishing marks, repeated headings and page numbers were excluded.
- The simplified stellar pressure expression is explicitly labelled as an order-of-magnitude model, while the hydrostatic differential equation is provided for scientific accuracy.
- The scanned source document is not included in public assets and no source-page images are displayed in the website.
